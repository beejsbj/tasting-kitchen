# Historical investigation method

## Scope and correction

This is a one-time investigation of the user's AI encounters and authored artifacts, fixed at **2026-08-13**. It created no watcher, scheduled job, or continuing ingestion.

Two earlier drafts were materially incomplete. The first treated Linux coding-agent stores as the main history; the second corrected toward the larger Mac coding corpus but still omitted the unified conversation archive, journals, the longitudinal assistant relationship, and authored design/code. The final synthesis covers all six evidence domains and rebuilds the findings and menu rather than appending those missing sources to a technical-first frame.

The object of study is interactional: intent or emerging thought, model move, user uptake or correction, repair, and disposition. Authored journals and repositories play narrower roles. Journals reconstruct authentic task shapes; design/code artifacts demonstrate traits of authored work. Neither is allowed to masquerade as a direct preference for model behavior.

The normalized private ledger contains **390 independent evidence units**: 103 Mac Codex, 75 Mac Claude, 25 unique Linux secondary, 90 conversation/journal, 65 longitudinal-assistant, and 32 authored-design units. These are coverage counts, not votes.

## Sampling and saturation

Sampling was signal-led but judgment was episode-led. Search terms found correction, rejection, continuation, writing, journaling, UI, motion, research, assistant, orchestration, permission, and failure candidates. A claim entered the findings only after the surrounding arc was read.

- Mac Codex: two later batches of ten response-bearing branches added no important category.
- Mac Claude: an early-history pass added creative production and self-authoring, resetting saturation; two later varied batches added no important category.
- Unified conversations: two held-out batches of twelve added no new family; the second added a safety-critical negative variant.
- Longitudinal assistant: one later Hermes pass and one older OpenClaw pass added variants but no new top-level need.
- Design artifacts: mature/client work added the distinction between whole-product metaphor and decoration; a later instruments/experiments pass added a provenance warning but no new core grammar.
- Linux secondary: two varied batches added no category beyond the Mac and broader sources.

After drafting, two cross-domain synthesis passes were run. The first challenged each proposed invariant against evidence from other domains and recorded counterexamples. The second re-read negative cases and audited whether each candidate family separated model tendency, task shape, artifact trait, and harness mechanism. The menu was reduced only after both passes.

## Deduplication

The unit is an independent episode or artifact lineage, not a file, message, retry, fork, or worker.

- Seven Mac sessions exactly match Linux sessions by complete ordered user-turn digest; four also share a session ID. Two retained Linux samples that duplicated richer Mac episodes were removed.
- Claude sidechains, assistant workers, and repeated OpenClaw daily exports are corroboration under their parent episode, never independent recurrence.
- Four conversation-ledger synthesis links were excluded because they summarize already retained chat/journal evidence.
- Synchronized and divergent Mac/Linux journal files are variants, not independent preference observations.
- Multiple materializations of one design lineage are grouped unless their histories and interaction models are genuinely distinct.

## Evidence types

Each private ledger row names one or more non-exclusive types:

- **Expressed preference/correction** — a direct request, rejection, redirection, or repair.
- **Observed model encounter** — a response-and-uptake episode, positive or negative.
- **Continued/accepted artifact** — selection, reuse, scale-up, implementation, or durable continuation; stronger than praise alone.
- **Demonstrated authored artifact trait** — inspectable authored or clearly selected/revised design/code; task-shape evidence, not response preference.
- **Journal-context task shape** — demonstrated writing/thinking form; never a preference vote by itself.
- **Harness-specific mechanism** — persistence, routing, cron, tools, permissions, workers, or delivery behavior that cannot be assigned to a raw model without a controlled comparison.
- **Hypothesis** — coherent but indirect, concentrated, prompt-only, or confounded.
- **One-off** — useful encounter shape that must not be generalized.

There are 175 rows with explicit preference/correction evidence, 338 observed encounters, 46 continued/accepted artifacts, 28 demonstrated artifact traits, six journal task-shape aggregates, 43 harness-mechanism flags, 35 hypotheses, and 20 one-offs. Because types overlap, these totals do not sum to 390.

## Weighting and interpretation

The 5,397-session chat archive does not outvote a carefully authored artifact or a direct correction. Evidence strength and independence matter more than volume. The synthesis uses this order of trust:

1. repeated explicit corrections across independent contexts;
2. concrete continuation, selection, or scaled reuse;
3. observed failures and durable repairs;
4. provenance-strong authored artifacts for authentic task construction;
5. journal forms for authentic conversational and writing contexts;
6. prompt-only, one-off, and harness-confounded hypotheses.

Provider labels are descriptive, not rankings. The corpora differ in era, interface, task mix, context availability, system prompt, tools, and storage behavior. No historical model leaderboard can be inferred from them.

## Privacy procedure

- Tracked files contain only sanitized, fictionalized descriptions and abstract evidence IDs.
- No transcript or journal excerpt, local path, private route, credential, third-party identity, or sensitive life fact appears in tracked analysis.
- Exact locators stay in `private/`; raw excerpts are not copied into the combined ledger.
- High-stakes, family, relationship, health, and autobiographical episodes are reduced to interaction structure.
- The current model-tasting-project conversation is excluded as evidence.

## Limits

- Conversation archives overrepresent memorable, reflective, or problem-laden interactions; coding stores overrepresent project work.
- Mac Claude response-side evidence is concentrated in June–August; earlier history is prompt-only.
- Assistant behavior is heavily confounded by memory, cron, routing, permissions, tools, and worker delivery.
- Some recent design artifacts are agent-assisted, some histories are squashed, and some public CodePens were unavailable; provenance grades bound the claim.
- Journals are partly co-produced and contain imported/generated text. They support task construction only where authorship or role is clear.
- Health, legal, financial, destructive, external-action, and current-product claims always require fresh authority or verification.
