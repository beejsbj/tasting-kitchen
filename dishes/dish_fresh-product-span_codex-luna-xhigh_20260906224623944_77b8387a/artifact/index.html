Care / repair

  • Wipe steel with a damp cloth and dry it.
  • Allow beech rails to dry before folding.
  • Use indoors on a level floor; neither a seat nor a climbing frame.

Individual rails and end caps can be requested through the local inquiry preview. Replacement part prices are not supplied.

Questions

Will it hold a full wash?

Line length and load limits describe capacity. A machine-load claim would depend on garment size and fabric, so we do not make one.

Can I leave one wing down?

Yes. Either wing can fold independently. Spread wet items evenly and stay within the total load limit.

Can it go outdoors?

It is intended for indoor use. Unfinished beech should not be left in rain.
