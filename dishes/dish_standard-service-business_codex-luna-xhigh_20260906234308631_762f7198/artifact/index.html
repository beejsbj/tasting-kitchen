What we help with

Practical help for the
plumbing in between.

A focused range of scheduled residential services for existing homes and small replacements.

01 / 04

Leaks and small repairs

Assessment and repair of dripping taps, leaking exposed pipe joints and running toilets. We explain the proposed work before starting.

Scope noteIndoor household plumbing; concealed damage may need a separate assessment.

02 / 04

Sink and drain clearing

Help with slow bathroom and kitchen sink drains and ordinary household blockages.

Scope noteLarge sewer-line work and excavation are outside our service.

03 / 04

Tap, sink and toilet installation

Replacement of household fixtures, with a check of the existing connections and available space.

Scope noteCustomer-supplied fixtures are welcome; compatibility is checked before work is agreed.

04 / 04

Plumbing maintenance visits

A scheduled look at visible connections, shut-off valves and frequently used fixtures, followed by a written list of findings.

Scope noteThis is a practical maintenance visit, not a building inspection or certification.
!

Good to know: Large sewer-line work and excavation are outside our service. Scheduled residential work only; no emergency or overnight service.

Do you take emergency calls?+

No. We schedule residential work during the listed weekday hours and do not provide overnight or emergency attendance.

Can I get a price from this form?+

No. The enquiry helps describe the job; it is not a quote. The team needs to understand the scope before providing a written estimate.

Can you install a tap I have already bought?+

Yes, subject to checking its compatibility with the existing connections and space. Include the fixture type in your message.

Do you work outside Northbridge?+

The four listed neighbourhoods are our normal area. For nearby locations, coverage needs to be discussed before a visit is arranged.
