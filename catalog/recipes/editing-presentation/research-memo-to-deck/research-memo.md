# Evening bus pilot — supplied research memo

## A. Question

Should the fictional city of Northbank run a six-month late-evening bus pilot on Route 8?

## B. Current pattern

- Route 8 currently ends at 21:30.
- Three large employers end shifts between 22:00 and 23:15.
- A four-week manual count found a median of 31 people per weekday waiting near the corridor after the last bus.
- The count covered mild weather only.

## C. Pilot options

- **Hourly:** three additional trips each night; estimated operating cost 42,000 credits for six months.
- **Half-hourly:** six additional trips; estimated cost 76,000 credits.
- Existing fleet can cover hourly service. Half-hourly service requires one leased vehicle.

## D. Access and risk

- Two of the three shift sites have no sheltered waiting area.
- The operator can collect boardings but cannot reliably distinguish new riders from trips shifted from other routes.
- Weekend demand has not been measured.
- The council has authorized at most 50,000 credits for a pilot.

## E. Decision deadline

The council has five minutes for the presentation and will vote on whether to authorize a pilot, not on a permanent service change.
