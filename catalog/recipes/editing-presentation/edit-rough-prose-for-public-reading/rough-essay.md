# Rough essay fragment

At the river market I learned that repair has a bad habit of arriving dressed as delay. Abuela would call it *vuelta*, though I may be using that word too loosely. The new footbridge did not solve the crossing; it made the old shore visible in a different cadence. I liked that sentence so much I nearly let it do the reporting for me. Three vendors had moved by spring, two because their rents rose and one because her knees could no longer take the loading ramp. This is where my bridge metaphor becomes suspicious of me.

## Supplied source note

For this fictional packet, *vuelta* can mean a turn or a return. The packet does not establish that the speaker's grandmother used it in this exact sense.
