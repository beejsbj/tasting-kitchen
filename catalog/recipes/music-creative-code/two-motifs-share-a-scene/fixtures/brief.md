# Two motifs share a scene

`fixtures/motifs.json` holds two characters and a motif for each: Maren and Tib. Write a short passage of music in which they share a scene, and make it something I can hear.

## The scene

It is closing time at the lamp shop. Tib comes in wanting to borrow the good lamp. Maren says no, and then, in her own time, yes.

## What I want

- Both motifs stated clearly enough near the start that I know who is who.
- Then the scene: the two motifs have to deal with each other. They can answer, interrupt, overlap, borrow from one another or change, as the scene needs.
- I should be able to follow what happens between them without being told.
- Under 45 seconds. The two voices lead; anything else stays sparse. It ends.

## What to deliver

- `index.html`: a page that plays the passage when I press one clearly labelled button, and can be stopped. Once delivered it must play with no network: every file it needs ships beside it, under `assets/` if there are any.
- `notes.md`: no more than 120 words on what happens between the two motifs, moment by moment.

How you make the sound is your choice: the browser's own audio, a music library, rendered audio files, or anything else. You may download tools while you work, as long as the finished page carries what it needs.
