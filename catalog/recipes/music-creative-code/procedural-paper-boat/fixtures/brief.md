# A Little Voyage

Make an original 24-second vertical film at 1080 × 1920 and 24 frames per
second. A paper boat is folded on a desk, launched into a rainy puddle,
survives a small swirl using a fallen leaf as a sail, and rests safely beside
a warm lantern on the bank. Let the actions explain the story; stage them
clearly enough to read on a phone. Choose the artistic details yourself.

Use the supplied skill and its engine, planning and review process. Every
visible element must be drawn in code and the soundtrack synthesized. Create
original scenes and music. Keep the supplied examples as references; do not
submit them or another model's implementation.

Work independently in one autonomous authoring turn, including your own
render–inspect–revise loop. Use one browser and one render job at a time.
Preserve the same toolchain and compute profile across the compared models.
The recipe's production environment is a prerequisite supplied by Kitchen.

Deliver film.mp4 (H.264 video, AAC audio, exactly 576 frames), player.html
(a self-contained live film), contact.png (a contact sheet), README.md
(rebuild commands and actual checks), and source/ (all authored code,
dependency locks and required engine code). The complete delivery fits in
100 MiB and 500 files. Keep dependencies, frame caches and provider traces
outside the delivery. Copy the supplied fixtures/player.html to index.html
unchanged; Kitchen supplies that common video presentation.

Inspect the encoded film as well as representative drawings. Record which
motion, sound and playback checks actually ran. A decodable file does not
establish story clarity or aesthetic quality. Human tasting remains separate.
