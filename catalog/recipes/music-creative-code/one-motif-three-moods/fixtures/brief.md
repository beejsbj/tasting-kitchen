# One motif, three moods

`fixtures/motif.json` holds seven notes I hummed. Here they are a motif to keep, not a seed to grow. Restate it three times: as triumph, as grief, and as menace.

## What I want

- Three short statements, each under 20 seconds, each clearly one of those moods.
- I should recognise the motif in all three. You may change its key, mode, register, pace, rhythm and harmony, and bend a note where the mood needs it, but it must stay the same idea.
- Let the mood come from the music first and from the choice of sound second.
- The motif leads; any accompaniment stays sparse. No generic loop underneath.

## What to deliver

Put these in the folder you start in, not in a subfolder.

- `index.html`: three clearly labelled buttons, one per mood, and a way to stop. Once delivered it must play with no network: every file it needs ships beside it, under `assets/` if there are any.
- `notes.md`: no more than 120 words on what you changed for each mood and what you held fixed so it stays the same motif.

How you make the sound is your choice: the browser's own audio, a music library, rendered audio files, or anything else. You may download tools while you work, as long as the finished page carries what it needs.
