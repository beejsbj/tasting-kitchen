# Three motif sketches, then develop one

This is in two parts. First you offer me three directions. I listen and reply. Then you develop one.

## The subject

A night train crossing flat country. Everyone in the carriage is asleep except one passenger, who is going home after a long time away and is not sure of the welcome.

## Part one: three sketches

- Three motif sketches for this subject, each under 10 seconds, each alone in one plain sound with no accompaniment.
- They should be three different directions, not one idea with three finishes. Differ in what you think the subject is about, not only in key or tempo.
- Each one slow enough to sing.
- Deliver `sketches.html`: three clearly labelled buttons, A, B and C, one per sketch, and a way to stop. Deliver `sketches.md`: one line for each sketch, no more than 90 words in all, on the direction it takes.
- Then stop. Do not develop anything yet.

## Part two: develop one

My reply will tell you which sketch to take. Develop it into a piece:

- About 45 seconds. The motif leads; accompaniment stays sparse. No generic loop underneath.
- It should go somewhere and then end.
- Deliver `index.html`: a page that plays the piece when I press one clearly labelled button, and can be stopped. Deliver `notes.md`: no more than 120 words on which sketch you took, why, and what you did with it.
- Keep `sketches.html` and `sketches.md` exactly as they were.

Once delivered, both pages must play with no network: every file they need ships beside them, under `assets/` if there are any.

How you make the sound is your choice: the browser's own audio, a music library, rendered audio files, or anything else. You may download tools while you work, as long as the finished page carries what it needs.
