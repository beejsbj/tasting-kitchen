# Grow a piece from a hummed seed

`fixtures/motif.json` holds seven notes I hummed. Treat them as a seed, not a finished tune. Make a short piece that grows out of them, and make it something I can hear.

## What I want

- About a minute of music.
- The hum is where the piece comes from. Take what you find in it (a turn of the melody, an interval, its fall, its rhythm) and follow it somewhere of your own. You may fragment it, extend it, answer it, reharmonise it, or let a new idea grow out of it.
- I should be able to hear the connection to the hum at least once, but the piece does not have to keep repeating it.
- Slow and voice-led, with sparse accompaniment. No generic loop underneath.
- It should go somewhere and then end.

## What to deliver

Put these in the folder you start in, not in a subfolder.

- `index.html`: a page that plays the piece when I press one clearly labelled button, and can be stopped. Once delivered it must play with no network: every file it needs ships beside it, under `assets/` if there are any.
- `notes.md`: no more than 120 words on what you heard in the hum, where you took it, and what to listen for.

How you make the sound is your choice: the browser's own audio, a music library, rendered audio files, or anything else. You may download tools while you work, as long as the finished page carries what it needs.
