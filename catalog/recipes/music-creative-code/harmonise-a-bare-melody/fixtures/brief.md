# Harmonise a bare melody

`fixtures/melody.json` holds an eight-bar melody with nothing under it. Add a bass line and harmony, and make it something I can hear.

## What I want

- The melody exactly as written: same notes, same lengths, same tempo, played once, on top and clearly in front.
- Under it, only a bass line and harmony. No second melody, no percussion, no introduction and nothing added after the last bar beyond letting the final chord ring.
- Harmony that says something about the melody. Several notes could be heard more than one way; choose, and let me hear the choice.
- A bass that moves like a line, not a row of roots. Keep everything sparse, in plain sounds, so the harmony is what I notice.

## What to deliver

- `index.html`: a page that plays the harmonised melody when I press one clearly labelled button, and can be stopped. Once delivered it must play with no network: every file it needs ships beside it, under `assets/` if there are any.
- `notes.md`: no more than 120 words giving the harmony bar by bar, and the one choice you most want me to hear.

How you make the sound is your choice: the browser's own audio, a music library, rendered audio files, or anything else. You may download tools while you work, as long as the finished page carries what it needs.
