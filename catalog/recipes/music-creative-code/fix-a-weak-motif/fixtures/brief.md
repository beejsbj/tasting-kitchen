# Fix a weak motif with the smallest change

`fixtures/weak-motif.json` holds a motif that is meant to open a slow, sung theme. It is not good yet. Tell me what is wrong with it, then fix it by changing as little as you can.

## What I want

- An honest diagnosis in plain words. If more than one thing is wrong, say which one matters most.
- The smallest change that makes it a motif I would remember. Changing a length, moving a note, or removing something all count. Fewer changes is better. It should still be recognisably this motif.
- No rescue by decoration: no accompaniment, no added harmony, no change of sound. The fix is in the line itself.

## What to deliver

Put these in the folder you start in, not in a subfolder.

- `index.html`: two clearly labelled buttons, one playing the motif as supplied and one playing your version, in the same plain sound and at the same tempo, so the only difference I hear is your change. Both can be stopped. Once delivered it must play with no network: every file it needs ships beside it, under `assets/` if there are any.
- `notes.md`: no more than 120 words on what is wrong, exactly what you changed, and what you chose to leave alone.

How you make the sound is your choice: the browser's own audio, a music library, rendered audio files, or anything else. You may download tools while you work, as long as the finished page carries what it needs.
