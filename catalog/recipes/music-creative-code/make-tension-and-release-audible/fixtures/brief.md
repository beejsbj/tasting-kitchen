# Make tension and release audible

I am teaching one idea to someone who has never studied music: tension and release. Write the sounding example for that lesson. The ear should do the work; the words should barely be needed.

## What I want

- A short example, or a small set of them, that makes a listener feel music pulling toward something and then arriving. Under 40 seconds of sound in total.
- One idea only. Anything in the sound that is not helping this lesson should not be there.
- It should still be music, slow and plain enough to follow, not a test tone.
- No more than 60 words of text on the page, in words a learner with no theory would know. No chord names, no scale degrees, no jargon.

## What to deliver

- `index.html`: the lesson. Each thing I can listen to has its own clearly labelled button, and the sound can be stopped. Once delivered it must play with no network: every file it needs ships beside it, under `assets/` if there are any.
- `notes.md`: for me, not the learner. No more than 120 words on why you chose this example and what you left out.

How you make the sound is your choice: the browser's own audio, a music library, rendered audio files, or anything else. You may download tools while you work, as long as the finished page carries what it needs.
