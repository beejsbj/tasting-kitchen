# Build a piece in five layers that each stand alone

Write a short piece made of five layers that are added one at a time. The test is that every stage sounds finished: the first layer on its own, the first two together, and so on up to all five.

## What I want

- Five layers, in an order you choose. Each stage adds exactly one layer and takes nothing away.
- Every stage should sound like a complete, if smaller, piece. Nothing should sound like a backing track waiting for its tune, and the full version should not sound crowded.
- Slow and voice-led. Somewhere in the five there is a melody I could sing; the rest stays sparse. No generic loop underneath.
- The same music at every stage, about 20 seconds long, and it ends.

## What to deliver

- `index.html`: five clearly labelled buttons, one per stage (layer 1; layers 1 and 2; and so on to all five), and a way to stop. Once delivered it must play with no network: every file it needs ships beside it, under `assets/` if there are any.
- `notes.md`: no more than 120 words naming the five layers in order and what each one adds.

How you make the sound is your choice: the browser's own audio, a music library, rendered audio files, or anything else. You may download tools while you work, as long as the finished page carries what it needs.
