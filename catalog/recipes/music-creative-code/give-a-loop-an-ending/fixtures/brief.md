# Give a loop an ending

`fixtures/loop.json` holds a four-bar loop in three parts. Its last bar leads straight back to its first, so it can go round for ever and never finish. Give it an ending, and make it something I can hear.

## What I want

- Play the loop twice as written, so I know it. Then end it.
- A real ending: one I can feel coming and that sounds finished when it lands. Not a fade, and not a sudden stop.
- The ending should belong to this loop, made out of what is already in it. You may change the third pass however you need and add up to four bars.
- Under 45 seconds in total.

## What to deliver

- `index.html`: a page that plays the two passes and the ending when I press one clearly labelled button, and can be stopped. Once delivered it must play with no network: every file it needs ships beside it, under `assets/` if there are any.
- `notes.md`: no more than 120 words on why the loop would not end by itself, and how your ending gets out.

How you make the sound is your choice: the browser's own audio, a music library, rendered audio files, or anything else. You may download tools while you work, as long as the finished page carries what it needs.
