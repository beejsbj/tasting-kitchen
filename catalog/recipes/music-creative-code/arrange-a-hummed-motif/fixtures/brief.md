# Arrange a hummed motif

`fixtures/motif.json` holds a seven-note motif I hummed. Make a short arrangement of it that I can hear.

## What I want

- About a minute of music built from this motif.
- The motif stays recognisable throughout. You may move it to another octave or key, and you may shape its rhythm, but keep its intervals.
- Slow and voice-led, with sparse accompaniment. No generic loop underneath it.
- It should go somewhere and then end.

## What to deliver

- `index.html`: a page that plays the piece when I press one clearly labelled button, and can be stopped. Once delivered it must play with no network: every file it needs ships beside it, under `assets/` if there are any.
- `notes.md`: no more than 120 words on what you did with the motif and what to listen for.

How you make the sound is your choice: the browser's own audio, a music library, rendered audio files, or anything else. You may download tools while you work, as long as the finished page carries what it needs.
