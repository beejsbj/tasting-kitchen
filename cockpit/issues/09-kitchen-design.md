# Redesign the gallery as a working test kitchen

Status: Ready for agent

Labels: `executor:codex`, `site:bjslab`

## Goal

Give the site a memorable kitchen character while keeping recipes, artifacts, and comparisons clearer than the theme.

## Context

The visual language can borrow from a working test kitchen: recipe cards, mise en place, order tickets, the pass, plates, shelves, stamps, and material surfaces. It should not become cartoon restaurant chrome or obscure the actual outputs.

## Done when

- a dedicated Design Lab pass derives the visual and interaction system after recipe-first information architecture is stable;
- the public menu, recipe brief, comparison surface, lineage filters, Cook control, and job states share one coherent system;
- artifact panes remain the largest and highest-contrast content;
- motion feels tactile and causal and honors reduced motion;
- responsive and accessibility checks pass;
- implementation includes a documented token/component/motion vocabulary.

## Constraints

- Depend on issue 04 and coordinate with issue 05.
- Do not copy Sitegeist's visual identity; borrow only the comparison clarity.
- Avoid novelty food illustrations unless they serve navigation or state.
